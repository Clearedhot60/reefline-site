Reefline Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Reefline is a single-player puzzle game. It has no accounts, no login and no chat, and it never shows an ad on its own. This page explains what information is used when you play.

What the game stores

Your progress, stars, pearls, hints, unlocked skins and colours, and settings are saved on your device only. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them. "Copy save code" in Settings copies your progress to your own clipboard so you can keep it or move it; it is never sent anywhere by the game.

Advertising

The only ads in Reefline are short videos that you choose to watch in return for a reward: a hint when you are out of hints, free pearls in the Shop, or doubling the pearls you just earned. Nothing plays unless you tap a button that offers an ad. Those ads are provided by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process information from your device, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking, other device identifiers, IP address (which indicates your general location), device and operating system type, and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this information is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in the Google Privacy Policy.

The first time you choose to watch an ad, your iPhone asks whether to allow tracking. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads still work but are not personalised using your advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In regions where the law requires it, the game also asks for your consent to personalised advertising, and you can change that choice later under Settings → Privacy choices in the game.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Sharing

If you share a Daily reef result, the game opens your iPhone's share sheet with a short text. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to send it.

What we collect

We collect nothing ourselves. Reefline has no analytics, we run no servers for it, and we do not receive personal information about players. The only third party is Google AdMob, for the ads you choose to watch, as described above.

Children

Reefline is not directed at children under 13, and we collect no personal information from anyone. Ads are requested with a "General audiences" content rating.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
