Reefline Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Reefline is a single-player puzzle game. It has no accounts, no login, no chat and no ads. This page explains what information is used when you play.

What the game stores

Your progress, stars, pearls, hints, unlocked skins and colours, and settings are saved on your device only. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them. "Copy save code" in Settings copies your progress to your own clipboard so you can keep it or move it; it is never sent anywhere by the game.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Sharing

If you share a Daily reef result, the game opens your iPhone's share sheet with a short text. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to send it.

What we collect

Nothing. Reefline has no analytics or advertising, we run no servers for it, and we do not receive personal information about players.

Children

Reefline collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
