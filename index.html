Reefline

Plant seeds. Restore the reef. A calm puzzle game, free on the App Store, with no ads.

Need help?

Email clearedhot08@gmail.com. Tell us your iPhone model and what happened, and we will get back to you.

How to play

Plant a seed on an empty cell and drag the way you want it to grow. Every seed you plant grows all the corals planted before it by one step, so the first seed grows the most. Fill every cell to restore the reef. The ? button in a level explains it again.

Purchases

Pearl packs and hint packs are sold through Apple. If a purchase did not arrive, close the game from the app switcher and open it again: Apple delivers unfinished purchases on launch. Unlimited hints can be brought back on a new phone with Shop → Restore purchases. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Keeping your progress

Progress is saved on your phone. To keep a copy or move it to another device, open Settings → Copy save code and keep the code somewhere safe; Load save code brings it back.

Sound and motion

Sound effects and music each have their own slider in Settings. Vibration, animations and coral numbers (helpful if colours are hard to tell apart) can be switched there too.

Privacy

Reefline collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
